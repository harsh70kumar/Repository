Dec 2024 – Dec 2025

Project Athlete 360

A meaningful chapter for physical education

For one dedicated year, Project Athlete 360 served as an independent space for sports analysis, athletic advocacy, and physical education. What began as a mission to elevate the conversation around holistic athletic development grew into a deeply rewarding journey.

At the close of 2025, the project officially reached its finish line.

While this specific publication has ceased operations and its original digital home has closed, the core belief remains unchanged: physical education is fundamental to human development. Thank you to everyone who read, engaged, and championed the work during our time active.

The conversation and work continue in new ways.
